Case study 01 · Jun 2026 – now
QordCurling AI Solutions · qord.in

One app for a homestay's bookings, money and guests.

Qord is a Property Management System (PMS) built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for Indian homestay and short-term rental hosts. It syncs calendars with Airbnb, Booking.com and MakeMyTrip, tracks every rupee of a stay, and gives guests a no-login portal and a WhatsApp concierge.

I joined as a founding developer and core team member. The parts below are the ones I built and own.

A guest portal that knows where you are in your stay.

Guests open one link: no app, no login. The home screen changes with the stage of the stay, so it always shows the next thing the guest needs.

  • Payment card pinned to the top until the stay and deposit are settled
  • Arrival day brings directions, door code and Wi-Fi to the front
  • ID upload accepts any photo format or PDF
  • Access to codes unlocks by check-in time and payment status

Inquiries, holds and confirmed stays, without double bookings.

Every booking moves through one model. Only the states that should block a date do, and confirmed stays push that block to every OTA calendar.

  • Inquiries never block availability
  • A hold reserves dates for a guest who is about to pay
  • The first real payment confirms the stay
  • Early checkout frees the unused nights and syncs them through Channex

Every rupee of a stay on one ledger.

The dashboard, payment links and guest portal all read the same numbers, so "Collected" and "Balance due" always agree.

  • The first payment of any size settles the advance and confirms the booking
  • The balance can never exceed the stay total
  • The security deposit is kept separate: refundable, not payment for the stay
  • Easebuzz links, UPI and cash entries, with versioned GST proforma PDFs

An LLM concierge that is not allowed to guess.

Guests ask questions on WhatsApp. The LLM picks a tool, and deterministic rules decide the answer: Allow, Deny or Needs confirmation from the host. Prices, availability and policies come from the database, never from the model.

  • Deterministic guardrails for availability, pricing, early check-in and late checkout
  • Idempotent actions, so a repeated message never double-books
  • Buttons that predict the next step instead of free-text prompts
  • Full guest lifecycle: confirmation, reminders, instant check-in, ID upload

Case study 02 · AICTE IDEA Lab · Mar – May 2026

SolarIQ reads a rooftop and prices the panels.

Give it an aerial image and a location. It finds the roof, removes trees, shadows and structures, pulls irradiance from PVGIS, and returns energy output, cost, savings and payback. The first version used an OpenCV contour pipeline; SolarIQ Pro replaced it with zero-shot segmentation from Meta's Segment Anything (SAM).

Input · CV Raman building, LNCT

Real output from SolarIQ Pro on the CV Raman building: Segment Anything finds the roof, OpenCV marks the obstacles, and what is left is 275.19 m² of usable rooftop.
